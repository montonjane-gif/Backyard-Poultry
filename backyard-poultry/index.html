About Backyard Poultry

Backyard Poultry is a small family farm in the Philippines dedicated to raising healthy, happy chickens the natural way. From our backyard to yours, every bird is cared for with love, fed well, and kept vaccinated so you get quality stock you can trust.

We specialize in native chickens, layers, broilers, and ornamental breeds, offering fair prices and reliable delivery to your doorstep.
